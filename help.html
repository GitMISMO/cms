The Basics
Start Here

The Certification Management System (CMS) is where every MISMO certification lives: the companies, their certifications, every open application and renewal, the invoices, and what happens next. It replaced the MACTS Smartsheet on October 25, 2026. Every MACTS automation is switched off and nothing is entered in MACTS any more.

Most of the work moves on its own. A stage recorded by an assessor, a payment seen by MISMO Money, a booking or date a company enters on its own page: each moves the certification on and sends the right email. What is left for a person is on the Action Queue.

  • MISMO staff work in the CMS: the Action Queue, raising invoices in Aptify, chasing follow-ups.
  • Assessors (Falcon, Actualize, Viant Eye, and MISMO's own team) work in the Assessor Portal: demos, findings, preliminary results, remediation.
  • MISMO's VP decides results on My Approvals, one click per decision.
  • MISMO's President countersigns Mark License Agreements in DocuSign.
  • Companies never log in. Each gets private links in its emails: the renewal page, the demo booking page, the remediation date page, and the application forms.

Every email to a company says to write to certifications@mismo.org; it never names a person. That mailbox is copied on everything that goes out.

Your Day: the Action Queue

The Action Queue opens with a row of figure boxes, one for each party whose move it is: Needs You, With the Company, With the Assessor, With Jonathan, Runs Itself (notices and DocuSign go out on their own), Chase Later, On Hold and Prospects. Select a box and its list opens below it, with a search and a sort. Every open certification is in exactly one box. The number beside Action Queue in the menu is the Needs You count.

Needs You lists only what needs MISMO, with the button that does it, grouped by task (or sorted by company, or most overdue first). The task buttons above the list show one kind of task at a time. The other boxes need nothing from you: each line says, in plain words, what that party does next and how long it has been at that step; a line turns red past 30 days. Needs You shows each company as a card, the same as the Assessor Portal: its monogram, where it is on the seven-step journey, the task and its buttons. The other boxes show one row per company: program, journey bar, what happens next and how long it has waited.

A company's record. Select a company to open its record on the right. The banner names the company and its certification; under it, the journey shows where it is and one box says whose move it is and what happens next. The rest is in tabs: Overview (follow-ups, moving it along, the assessor and notes), Money (fee, invoice, payment), The Certification (anniversary, certification script, Mark License, history) and The Company (contacts and website). The full record has Overview, Mark License, History and The Company.

The Process
An Initial Certification, Step by Step
  1. Application. The company fills in the program's application form on resources.mismo.org. It lands on the board as a new application (or staff add one with + New Application: type the applicant's contact, drop the Aptify invoice PDF and the order number and total are read off it, then Add and Send the Invoice emails it to the applicant from certifications@mismo.org, PDF attached, certifications@ copied, on the next run in business hours). Any initial certification's record has the same Email the Invoice box. The Application Agreement goes out through DocuSign; the company and MISMO's President sign it.
  2. Invoice and payment. MISMO raises the invoice in Aptify and records the order number. MISMO Money reports the payment each morning; a payment that matches is recorded on its own and the certification moves on. Anything that does not match waits under "Paid, and Held for You" on the Invoices page.
  3. Welcome and demo booking. Once paid, the company is sent its private booking page with the assessor's open times. It picks a time and names who attends; the demo is booked straight away and everyone is told. Weekly reminders follow until it books (four, then MISMO is told).
  4. Demonstration or interview. RON, eClosing and eVault have a demonstration against the certification script; Product, Consultant, FRAME and AI Governance have an interview. If the assessor does not record the outcome, they are reminded.
  5. Artifacts. The assessor asks for what is missing; the company is reminded weekly.
  6. Preliminary results. The assessor submits them in the Assessor Portal, with or without findings. They go to the VP on My Approvals.
  7. Decision. The VP approves (one click), sends the findings for remediation, asks the assessor a question, or records Not Certified.
  8. Remediation (only with findings). The company is sent the items and a link to give the date it expects to finish. If the date passes it is followed up, and 30 days after it the VP and assessor are told. When the company is done, the assessor submits it and it comes back to the VP.
  9. Approved. The company and assessor are told, the anniversary is set, and the Mark License Agreement goes out through DocuSign on its own (the licensee signs first, then MISMO's President).
  10. Certified. Once the license is signed. The license never holds up the certification itself.

Each stage has an owner shown on the board: the company, the assessor, MISMO, the VP, or the company and President (signatures). An application that goes quiet is flagged for follow-up after 30 days. A company that is not going ahead is moved to Prospects.

Renewals

A certification renews every year on the same day, whether the renewal finishes early or late. The program agreements allow a grace period after the anniversary (60 days for RON, eClosing and eVault).

  1. Invoice. The weekly Invoices to Raise email (the first working day of each week, to certifications@mismo.org) and the Action Queue list each renewal invoice when it is due: 14 days before the first notice, so it is on the renewal page before the company is told. Raise it in Aptify, drop the PDF on the line, record the order number.
  2. Notices. The renewal notices go on their own, counted off the anniversary (table below), plus a weekly invoice reminder while payment is outstanding. They stop once the renewal moves on.
  3. The renewal page. Each notice carries the company's private renewal page link. There it confirms its details, answers the certification script questions (prefilled from last year), signs the attestation(s), downloads the invoice, and can open what it sent last year. Downloads ask for a code emailed to the contact on file.
  4. Payment. Seen by MISMO Money and recorded on its own.
  5. The rest follows the same steps as an initial: certification script prepared on its own for RON, eClosing and eVault, demo booked by the company, results, VP decision, Mark License.

Demos and Remediation: the Company's Own Pages

Demo booking (resources.mismo.org/forms/schedule/). Each certification waiting to schedule has a private page listing its assessor's open times: at least two working days ahead, within six weeks, never on a MISMO holiday, never double-booked. Falcon's times come from its weekly windows; Actualize publishes a look-only calendar, so the company offers up to three times and Actualize confirms one. Once booked the certification moves to Demo Scheduled and everyone is emailed.

Remediation target date (resources.mismo.org/forms/remediation/). Each certification in remediation has a private page where the company gives the date it expects to finish, and can change it. The date drives the follow-ups. A new round of remediation starts with no date.

Both pages are served by the same Power Automate flow ("MISMO Demo Booking - Serve"). Nothing on them moves until the system checks it, usually within two minutes.

Approvals and the Mark License

My Approvals is the VP's page: every result, remediation and scope decision waiting on MISMO, each with the assessor's findings (or No Findings). Approve is one click with an optional note. Sending for remediation, asking the assessor a question or Not Certified each need a note, which goes to the right people. The VP is reminded every three days while something waits.

Mark License Agreement. Goes out through DocuSign on its own after approval, to the signer named on the company record, then to MISMO's President. A certification's Mark License section shows where it stands (Queued, Sent, Opened, Executed) and the signed copies MACTS held, which are filed by company in SharePoint under Certification Applications > Mark License Agreements. Nothing is recorded by hand: DocuSign is checked three times every weekday, a signed agreement moves the certification to Certified, and the signed copy files itself in that same folder and is linked on the certification. The only exceptions are the few licences MACTS sent before DocuSign was connected here; their Mark License section says "This one needs you" with a button to record that it came back. If there is no signer email, it shows on the Action Queue as Needs an Email.

Assessors

Each firm works in the Assessor Portal (resources.mismo.org/assessors/): its engagements, the certification scripts to download, its availability, and every step it records (demo done, findings, preliminary results, remediation). Each step moves the certification and emails the company. MISMO can see each firm's view from the Assessor Portal page in the CMS.

Emails

Every email uses the MISMO email shell and goes from the certifications mailbox, with certifications@mismo.org copied. Nothing goes outside business hours (9:00 to 5:00 Eastern on MISMO working days); anything due waits for the next working morning. An email whose wording has not been approved goes to certifications@mismo.org marked [Held for Review] instead. The Email Playbook shows each one, lets you change the wording and send yourself a test. Sent Emails shows everything that went.

Behind the Scenes
MISMO Money

From MISMO Money: each morning it sends the Aptify payments it has loaded. A payment that matches one invoice exactly is recorded and the certification moves on; anything else waits on the Invoices page for a person to Record It.

To MISMO Money: the date each certification was approved, which is when MISMO Money recognizes the revenue and the assessor's fee. Written the first time the approval is seen and never changed.

The foot of the CMS menu shows when each last happened. A green dot is current; amber means older than expected (more than three days for payments, thirty for approvals).

Who at a company gets which email. Each company record holds a Primary Contact, an Invoice Contact and an Authorized Signer (Companies, then Company Details). Certification emails go to the primary contact; invoices and payment reminders to the invoice contact; the Mark License Agreement, the approval and the renewal notices also to the authorized signer. If the invoice contact or signer is missing, that email goes to the primary contact. Until a company's primary contact is saved in the CMS, the people MACTS wrote to keep receiving emails too.

Assessors are emailed only when they are asked to do something: confirm a time, send the invitation, receive artifacts or remediation evidence, answer a question, or record a result.

If Something Goes Wrong

The system checks itself. Every working morning, after the renewal notices, it looks for a job that has failed, the automatic runs having stopped, MISMO Money not sending payments, and records that do not fit together (a certification in assessment with no assessor, an approval with no Mark License record, a company with no contact email). If it finds anything it emails certifications@mismo.org once that day, with a System Health Check subject, in plain words. No email means everything was in order.

Nothing is lost if something is down. If the email flow is down, a renewal notice goes out on the next run within a week, and stage emails are sent once it is back. If two jobs save at the same moment, the second settles it record by record instead of failing.

  • An email did not go. Check it was inside business hours, and Sent Emails. A [Held for Review] copy in certifications@mismo.org means its wording is not approved yet, or someone it is meant for has no email on file: add the email on the Companies page and it is released.
  • A company says its page does not open. Ask for the link exactly as emailed. The renewal and booking pages depend on their Power Automate flows being on.
  • A payment was not picked up. Look under Paid, and Held for You on the Invoices page; the reason is written there. Check the MISMO Money dot at the foot of the menu.
  • A record is wrong. Most fields are corrected in place on the certification or company record; every change keeps who made it. A stage move can be undone straight after (Ctrl+Z).
