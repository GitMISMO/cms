Editing and Testing

Open an email, change the Heading, Subject Line or Email Body, then press Save Wording. The change is used for every send of that email from then on, and the card shows who last edited it and when. Restore Original Wording puts back the wording the email started with.

Send Me a Test sends that email to you alone, filled in with a real certification from the board as the sample, marked [Test] in the subject. It uses whatever is in the boxes at that moment, so you can change the wording, test it, and only save when you are happy. Nobody else receives it and nothing is recorded as sent. Tests go out at any hour; the real emails wait for business hours.

A test normally arrives within two minutes. The card shows when it has gone. Who gets each email and when it sends are set in the system itself; changing those is a change to the system, not to the wording.

Saving needs Edit access to the Certification Management System. With View access the page is read only.
